# Paper

A preprint draft (bioRxiv-ready) for the Labwright benchmark.

- `manuscript.md` — full manuscript: intro, system (propose / calculate /
  verify), benchmark (24 gold organ-on-chip goals, bare LLM vs Labwright on
  `deepseek-v4-flash` and `deepseek-v4-pro`), reverse verification of published
  protocols, discussion and limitations.

All numbers in the Results section come from the committed benchmark outputs:

- `results/eval_flash.json`, `results/eval_pro.json` (regenerated by
  `python -m eval.run_benchmark`)
- `results/eval_verify_batch.json` (regenerated by `python -m eval.run_verify_batch`)
